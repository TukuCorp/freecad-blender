## Most important things for a structural-safety reviewer
1. Pile design: 250 x 250 RC pressed piles, 24 m long (3 x 8 m segments, two splices), 45 t working capacity and 90 t / 67.5 t jacking loads (note 4 to 6, sheets 9983/9985); no geotech report, no cover and no stop criterion beyond the pressure; about 31 piles by my count with 7 single-pile caps (M4, M6, M7, M8/M8a) whose columns are eccentric to the pile (700 mm), so pile moment or tie-beam restraint must be checked; no ĐK grade-beam sections are shown at all (sheet 9981).
2. Column sizes: C1/C2 only 200 x 350 (ground to Lầu 1), reducing to 200 x 300 and 200 x 200 (2Ø16+2Ø16 at the top storeys), Cc only 200 x 200 with 4Ø14 for a 5-storey house; starter bars 8Ø20 / 6Ø20 / 4Ø14 and Ø6a100-200 ties (sheet 9982); check capacity against pile loads and the axial demand, and the lack of lap/anchorage lengths anywhere.
3. Drawing inconsistencies to resolve with the contractor/designer: M1 quantity 10 vs layout (9983); cap top levels -0.500 (M1/M2) vs -0.050 (M3/M7) vs -1.550/-1.650 (lift caps); M5 -2.250 vs the 750 dim (9985); M8/M8a both "02" and section 8-8 missing (9986); ribbed-steel Ra 2800 (foundation) vs 2700 (stairs); slab thickness 100 / 120 / 150 across sheets; concrete mác 300 in foundation/columns to Lầu 1 then mác 250.
4. Lift pit (sheet 9987): 1.6 m deep, 200 walls, slab 150, beams 300 x 500 and Ø10a150 mesh; no waterproofing, no sump, no lift capacity/stops/speed given, level mismatch -1.550 vs -1.650; hoist hook is a single Ø25 loop in a 120 slab with no stated rated load (sheet 9988); the "tầng hầm" 2-layer note is copied to a roof sheet.
5. Stairs (0003/0004/0005): 100 thick flights with Ø10a120 main bars and Ø8a150, 21 risers per storey, landings only on DS3/ĐK3 beams; no headroom, no handrail loads, no cover and no landing slab detail on the sheets; the central 500 void between flights has no edge detail shown.
