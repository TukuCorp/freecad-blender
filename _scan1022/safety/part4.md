- **Basis:** JUDGEMENT for the specific building; the requirements are engineering norms and no clause is quoted, because **the Vietnamese MEP standards (QCVN 32:2024/BXD on electrical safety, TCVN 2737 on lightning protection and earthing) were NOT FOUND** in this review.

- **Evidence label:** VERIFIED (the pit and shaft dimensions quoted from the transcripts; QCVN 06 3.4.6 and ADB 2.7/2.13 read from the fetched documents). - **Severity:** MEDIUM
- **Where:** `transcript-architecture.md`, IMG_9972: *"Nơi để xe (parking) +-0.000"*, *"Sân trước (front yard) -0.200"*; IMG_9977: *"this floor has no rooms except a stair/lift lobby (5200 long, 3200 + 1800 + 200)"* — i.e. **floor 5 has no habitable room at all**, only the stair/lift lobby and two doors to the terraces. `transcript-foundation-stairs.md`: the stair is the only vertical circulation, with three different riser heights and winders.
- **Issue:** The house can be made to age in place **only via the lift**, and the lift's accessibility credentials are entirely undocumented: the **clear door width is not stated** (the plan shows the door gap as *"300 + 1000 + 300"*, i.e. 1000 mm nominal between the reveals, but the clear opening after the door leaves is not stated), the **car dimensions are not stated**, and **the stair — the alternative if the lift fails or the occupant cannot use it — has winders and a 176.2 mm riser at the bottom.** Meanwhile the street-to-door route has a 200 mm step (S-14), the WCs are 1600 × 2100 with 700 mm doors (S-22), and there is no level-access route anywhere.
- **Why it matters:** A 5-storey house for a family that will age is a specific design brief, and the drawing set addresses it only implicitly through the presence of a lift. The consequences are concrete: if the lift needs replacing at 15–20 years, the shaft must fit a modern replacement — and the shaft is 1600 mm clear with a plant room whose internal height is ~1300 mm, neither of which is documented against any supplier's requirement. If the lift fails, the only alternative is a stair with winders and a 176.2 mm bottom riser, which is not a usable accessible route for an elderly person. And a 200 mm step at the front door is the first thing an elderly visitor fails at.
- **Basis:** UK ADB **para. 3.107** — *"Final exits should not present a barrier for disabled people. Where the route to a final exit does not include stairs, a level threshold and, where necessary, a ramp should be provided."* UK ADK (2013) **para. 1.16** — for dwellings, in exceptional circumstances of severely sloping plots, entrance-storey stairs may have a minimum width of 900 mm (this house's entrance stair is 1250–1350 mm, comfortably above). Approved Document M (accessibility) is the applicable document for the approach, the lift and the WC clear space, and was **NOT FOUND** in this review — no clause from it is quoted.
- **Basis class:** BEYOND-LOCAL (ADB 3.107, ADK 1.16) and JUDGEMENT (the ageing-in-place reading)
- **Fix:** Treat the house as an **"ageing in place" project** and put the following in the tender: (i) the lift's **clear door opening (≥ 900 mm) and car clear dimensions (≥ 1100 × 1400 mm)**, stated in the drawing set and confirmed by the supplier in writing; (ii) a **step-free route from the street to the lift lobby on the ground floor**, with the front door threshold removed or ramped (S-14); (iii) a **ground-floor WC with an accessible layout** — the current ground-floor WC is the rear 1600 × 2100 unit, which is the worst possible place for it; (iv) a **bedroom and a full bathroom on the lửng (mezzanine)** adjacent to the lift, so a future resident who cannot use the stairs has a step-free level with a bed and a WC; the lửng is currently a single 4400 mm-deep living room with a WC at its rear — this is the most valuable single change available to the house; and (v) handrails on both sides of every flight, a contrasting nosing, and a non-slip finish on all treads (S-21). The point of (iv) is that it converts the mezzanine from a feature room into a fallback living floor.
- **Who decides:** owner (the brief), architect (the re-plan), lift supplier (the dimensions), MEP engineer (the accessible WC and the grab-rail backing).
- **Evidence label:** VERIFIED (the levels, the floor-5 lobby-only plan and the stair risers quoted from the transcripts; ADB 3.107 and ADK 1.16 read from the fetched documents). Approved Document M is **NOT FOUND**, so no accessibility dimension is quoted from it; the lift door and car dimensions are **NOT FOUND** because no supplier has been appointed.

**Topic 9 — no issue found / cannot assess:**

- **Bathroom clear space: cannot assess** — no WC layouts are in the set (S-22).
- **Step-free route to the lift: no issue found within the house** — the lift is on every level, so there is a step-free route within the house from the lift lobby to each floor's landing, subject to the level differences; the issue is the **approach** through the front door (S-14) and the **car and door dimensions** (S-54).
